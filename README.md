# Green Aid Foundation

Redesign the website for Green Aid Foundation (https://greenaidfoundation.org/ with current logo at https://greenaidfoundation.org/assets/images/Green_Aid_Foundation_1.png). It's an environmental NGO based in Kenya focused on harmonious coexistence between humans and nature.

Incorporate the core pillars:

1. The Green Project: reforestation, indigenous tree nurseries, agroforestry, and combating tree loss from tobacco farming. Include a Tree Seedling Request form.
2. The Yellow Project: apiculture and beehive bio-fences along reserve borders to eliminate human-elephant conflict while generating farmer income through honey.
3. The Blue Project: wetlands, riverbank restoration, bamboo buffers, and water catchment protection (such as Orarwe Dam).
4. Community & Education programs: Edu-Kids climate literacy and women/youth empowerment (e.g. Taranganya camp).

Incorporate the design tokens and visual system:

- Palette: Deep Highland Forest Green (#19432B) as primary brand color, warm off-white canvas (#FBFBF9 / #F4F1EA), and distinctive pillar accents (Green #15803D, Honey/Savanna Gold #D97706, Riparian Blue #0284C7).
- Typography & Layout: Crisp, modern editorial styling with clear metrics and impact counters.
- Donation & Support hub: Transparent contribution options including M-Pesa Paybill and international giving.
- Ensure the redesign is clean, responsive, dignified, and premium while staying deeply authentic to their grassroots conservation mission.
